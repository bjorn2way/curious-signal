Also in the mix, from r/LocalLLaMA: Liquid AI has released LFM2.5-2.6B, a 2.69 billion parameter model designed for local AI deployment.

The model features a 128K context window and supports tool calling, with post-training specifically optimized for multi-step agent workflows.

The official Q4KM GGUF quantization is approximately 1.67 GB in size and is compatible with llama.cpp.

Performance benchmarks indicate the model can run at 30 tokens per second on a mobile phone and 113 tokens per second on a CPU, highlighting its efficiency for edge devices.

This release targets users who need capable local models that are significantly smaller and more accessible than massive alternatives.
