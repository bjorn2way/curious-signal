### Anthropic is turning Claude Code’s auto mode on by default

Anthropic will make "auto mode" the default for Claude Code Pro, Max, and Team accounts starting August 14, 2026. In this mode, the AI proceeds with tasks without seeking human approval for each step, intervening only for actions deemed "irreversible, destructive, or aimed outside your environment." Key details include: * **Safety Performance:** A study of 1,053 paid testers showed auto mode caught 89% of harmful actions, compared to only 13.6% for human review. This disparity is attributed to "habitual" human approval, where users approved 97% of permission prompts. * **Additional Safety Features:** The update includes prompt injection screening and customizable hard deny rules to prevent data exfiltration. * **User Adoption:** Boris Cherny, Head of Claude Code, stated the team uses auto mode exclusively and finds it superior to manual permission prompts. ([Anthropic is turning Claude Code’s auto mode on by default](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/09/anthropic-is-turning-claude-codes-auto-mode-on-by-default/)).

### KLQ: Training-free measured rotation quantization. Beats all training-free rotation-based quantization methods on W4A4KV4-bits. Llama 3.2 1B KLQ-quantized beats SpinQuant and gets close to ReSpinQuant without GPTQ/LDLQ rounding.

**Overview** KLQ Kullback-Leibler Quantization is a training-free quantization method that optimizes bit allocation across LLM embedding spaces by leveraging their inherent anisotropy. Unlike uniform quantization or rotation-based methods e.g., QuaRot, SpinQuant that attempt to isotropize the space, KLQ identifies important directions via eigendecomposition and allocates bits using a water-filling algorithm based on causally measured damage KL divergence . **Methodology** 1. **Geometry Analysis**: For each weight matrix, KLQ computes the covariance of activation samples from a calibration set and performs eigendecomposition to identify principal directions eigenvectors and their variances eigenvalues . 2. **Causal Damage Measurement**: It perturbs activations along each eigenvector direction by one standard deviation and measures the resulting KL divergence from the original model’s output distribution. This provides a causal measure of each direction's importance. 3. **Bit Allocation**: The "price" of quantizing a direction is defined as $p_i = ||Wv_i||^2 \times KL_i$. Bits are allocated per direction using a water-filling algorithm to minimize total perplexity under a bit-width budget. 4. **Quantization Backends**: The method supports interchangeable quantization backends.
