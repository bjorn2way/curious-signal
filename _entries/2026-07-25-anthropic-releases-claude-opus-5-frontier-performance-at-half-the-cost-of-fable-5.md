## Verdict

On July 24, 2026, Anthropic released Claude Opus 5, a model that significantly outperforms its predecessor (Opus 4.8) and approaches the capabilities of the frontier-tier Claude Fable 5 at approximately half the cost. It maintains the same token pricing as Opus 4.8 ($5M input / $25M output) but delivers higher accuracy and efficiency in agentic coding, scientific research, and knowledge work. The release includes major shifts in context engineering best practices and introduces new API features like mid-conversation tool changes. ([Claude Opus 5](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-opus-5)).

## Findings

- Opus 5 is the default model on Claude Max and strongest on Claude Pro. ([Claude Opus 5](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-opus-5))
- Performance on CursorBench 3.2 is within 0.5% of Fable 5 at half the cost. ([Claude Opus 5](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-opus-5))
- ARC-AGI 3 scores are 3x higher than the next-best model. ([Claude Opus 5](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-opus-5))
